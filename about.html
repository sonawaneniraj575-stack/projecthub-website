About ProjectHub

Practical software, made easier to start.

ProjectHub is a student-focused software studio helping engineering and computer science students turn ideas into practical software.

What ProjectHub does

ProjectHub provides ready-to-use software projects, custom websites, full-stack applications, project documentation and setup or explanation support where offered.

Who it is for

The catalogue is designed for CS, IT, ENTC, BCA, MCA and engineering students looking for a practical starting point for a software project.

Why it exists

Good project work should be clear enough to explore, useful enough to demonstrate and flexible enough to discuss. ProjectHub focuses on modern technologies, understandable scope and a direct support conversation.

What we do not promise

Academic requirements and approval differ across institutions. ProjectHub does not promise grades, marks, approval or any specific academic outcome.
