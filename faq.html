Questions, answered

Before you choose a project.

Here is the practical information to help you decide what to ask next.

Do you provide source code?

Source code is provided when it is part of the specific project or agreement. Confirm what is included before purchase.

Can I request a custom project?

Yes. Share your idea, requirements and preferred technology through WhatsApp for a scope discussion and quote.

Can projects be modified?

Customization depends on the requirements, project structure and agreed scope. Discuss changes before proceeding.

Do you provide project explanation?

Setup and explanation support can be available depending on the project and agreed deliverables.

Do you provide deployment help?

Optional deployment and hosting assistance can be discussed for suitable projects.

How do I purchase a project?

Open a project, use the Get Project button and discuss the project, inclusions and next steps on WhatsApp. Payment and delivery are handled manually.

Do you accept online payments?

The website does not process online payments. Ask about the current manual payment process through WhatsApp.

Can I see a project before purchasing?

Ask about available demonstrations or previews. What can be shown depends on the project.

Do you provide refunds?

Refund eligibility depends on delivery status, customization, whether custom work has started and the agreement made before purchase. See the Refund Policy.

Are projects guaranteed to receive academic approval?

Academic requirements and approval vary by institution and faculty. ProjectHub does not guarantee academic approval, marks, grades or acceptance.
